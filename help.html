How to use the Classroom Layout Planner

Your work saves itself as you go. You can close the page and come back later.

Add and move furniture

  1. In the Add furniture list on the left, click the furniture you want. It appears in the middle of the room. Student tables and desks bring their chairs with them: along the long side of a desk, all the way around a round table, and along the curved side of a kidney table.
  2. Drag it to where it belongs.
  3. The number beside each piece is how many are in the room. Click + to add another or − to take one away.
  4. To turn it, click it. Small buttons appear just above it. Click Rotate.
  5. To change its size, click it, then click Edit above it. Type the width and depth. Leave Resize every … ticked to change all pieces of that kind, or untick it to change only the selected piece.
  6. The same Edit box changes the name, color and shape, and how many chairs come with each new one. Remove from the list takes that kind of furniture away completely; click it twice.
  7. To remove it, click it, then click Delete above it.
  8. Made a mistake? Click Undo, under the lists on the left.

Add a new kind of furniture

  1. At the bottom of the Add furniture list, click + New furniture.
  2. Type a name and choose a color, then click Add.
  3. One appears in the room with its Edit box open. Type its size, then drag it into place.

Change the room

  1. To move a wall, drag it, or drag the number on it. A wall close to lining up with another snaps into line.
  2. To set an exact wall length, click the wall and type the length in the box beside it.
  3. To add a whiteboard, window, door or other item, click it under Add to the Wall. Drag it along the walls to the right spot. Drag the square at either end to make it longer or shorter.
  4. Click a board, door or window to see its box. There you can change its label and size, or click Delete.
  5. Each door gets a red Keep clear area in front of it. To add one in front of a board or window, tick Keep clear in front in its box and type how far. The area moves with its door or board.
  6. To start the outline again, click Draw outline at the top. Click each corner in order, then click the first corner again to finish.
  7. Is your room a mirror image of the plan? Click Flip at the top, then Flip left–right or Flip front–back.
  8. Click Show at the top to turn things on the plan on or off. For example, untick Wall lengths to hide the numbers on the walls.

Move several pieces at once

  1. Drag across an empty part of the floor to draw a box around the pieces.
  2. Drag any one of them. The others move with it.

Read the plan

  1. Each square on the floor is 1 foot.
  2. Click a piece to see how much open space is around it.
  3. Look at the bottom of the page. It shows how many seats you have and whether anything needs checking.
  4. Click things to check to see the list. Red means two things overlap or something is in the way. Yellow means a walkway is narrower than 2 feet.
  5. The number beside each piece in the furniture list is how many are in the room.
  6. The gray Holding area under the room is a place to park furniture while you rearrange. Furniture there is not counted.

Layouts

Each layout is a whole plan: the room, its walls, boards and doors, and the furniture. Your work saves itself as you go.

  1. To name the layout you are in, type in Layout name. Click Save any time to save straight away.
  2. To switch layouts, choose one from the Layout menu at the top.
  3. To try a new arrangement in the same room, choose + New layout… from that menu. It starts with the same room and no furniture.
  4. To try a change without losing what you have, choose Duplicate this layout, then change the copy.
  5. To remove a layout, choose Delete this layout….

Layouts stay on this computer, in this browser. To keep a copy somewhere else, use Download.

Download a backup

  1. Click Download at the top.
  2. A picture of your layout is saved to your computer.
  3. Keep that file. It is your backup.

You can email the file as an attachment. Do not take a screenshot of it or paste it into a document. The copy would lose your layout.

Upload a backup

  1. Click Upload at the top.
  2. Choose a picture that was made with Download.
  3. It opens as a new layout, and appears in the Layout menu.

Print

  1. Click Print at the top.

Set up a different room

  1. Choose + New layout… from the Layout menu, and give it a name.
  2. Drag the walls into place, or click a wall and type its length. Or click Draw outline and click each corner of the room.
  3. Move, add or delete boards, doors and windows to match the new room.
