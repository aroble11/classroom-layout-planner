How to use the Classroom Layout Planner

Your work saves itself as you go. You can close the page and come back later.

Add and move furniture

  1. In the Add furniture list on the left, click the furniture you want. It appears in the middle of the room.
  2. Drag it to where it belongs.
  3. The number beside each piece is how many are in the room. Click + to add another or − to take one away.
  4. To turn it, click it. Small buttons appear just above it. Click Rotate.
  5. To change its size, click it, then click Size above it. Type the width and depth. Leave Resize every … ticked to change all pieces of that kind, or untick it to change only the selected piece.
  6. To remove it, click it, then click Delete above it.
  7. Made a mistake? Click Undo, under the lists on the left.

Add a new kind of furniture

  1. At the bottom of the Add furniture list, click + New furniture.
  2. Type a name and choose a color, then click Add.
  3. One appears in the room with its size box open. Type its size, then drag it into place.

Change the room

  1. To move a wall, drag it, or drag the number on it. A wall close to lining up with another snaps into line.
  2. To set an exact wall length, click the wall and type the length in the box beside it.
  3. To add a whiteboard, window, door or other item, click it under Add to the room. Drag it along the walls to the right spot. Drag the square at either end to make it longer or shorter.
  4. Under Show, untick Wall lengths to hide the numbers on the walls.

Move several pieces at once

  1. Drag across an empty part of the floor to draw a box around the pieces.
  2. Drag any one of them. The others move with it.

Read the plan

  1. Each square on the floor is 1 foot.
  2. Click a piece to see how much open space is around it.
  3. Look at the bottom of the page. It shows how many seats you have and whether anything needs checking.
  4. Click things to check to see the list. Red means two things overlap or something is in the way. Yellow means a walkway is narrower than 2 feet.
  5. The number beside each piece in the furniture list is how many are in the room.
  6. Furniture in the gray strip under the room is spare. It is not counted.

Save a layout

  1. Type a name in Layout name.
  2. Click Save.
  3. To go back to it later, click Saved layouts, find it in the list, and click Open.

Saved layouts stay on this computer, in this browser. To keep a copy somewhere else, use Download.

Download a copy

  1. Click Download a copy at the top.
  2. A picture of your layout is saved to your computer.
  3. Keep that file. It is your backup.

You can email the file as an attachment. Do not take a screenshot of it or paste it into a document. The copy would lose your layout.

Upload a copy

  1. Click Upload a copy at the top.
  2. Choose a picture that was made with Download a copy.
  3. The room and layout appear.
  4. Click Save to keep it.

Print

  1. Click Print at the top.

Set up your own room

Most people do this once.

  1. Click Room setup at the top.
  2. Type a name for the room.
  3. Type the room's width and length, then click Make rectangle.
  4. If your room is not a rectangle, click Draw outline. Click each corner in order. Click the first corner again to finish.
  5. To set an exact wall length, click the wall. Type the length in the box that appears next to it, and press Enter.
  6. To add a board, window or door, click it under Add to a wall. Then drag it to the right spot. Drag the square at either end to make it longer or shorter, or type the size in the box next to it.
  7. To add something that never moves, like a countertop or sink, click Fixture. Give it a name and a size, then drag it into place.
  8. Under Furniture for this room, click a piece of furniture to change its size or how many you have.
  9. Click Done.

Is your room a mirror image of one you already set up? Click Flip left–right.

Use more than one room

  1. Click the Room menu at the top.
  2. Choose + New room…
  3. Use the same menu to switch between rooms.
